Everything in them is from the editor's general knowledge, not opened sources, and each needs research before it can become a case.

## Why these ten

Selection followed the Stage 3 brief: a queer-history case, an everyday-life case, quantitative data, non-European regions, a surprising coexistence and a calendar problem. The test was whether a dated, openable source existed for a year-level boundary. That filter favoured topics with Wikipedia coverage, which is itself a bias recorded in AUDITS.md.
