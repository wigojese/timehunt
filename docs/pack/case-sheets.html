A political turning point. Oceania. modern.

In which year did New Zealand pass the law giving women the right to vote in parliamentary elections?

Answer 1893. Royal assent to the Electoral Act 1893 (19 September 1893) Precision: day known (19 September); year used. Calendar: Gregorian.

The year the Act received royal assent. Women first voted in the general election of 28 November 1893, the same year. Women could vote before they could stand for Parliament; this case is about the vote. Event: assent to the Act. Not the first vote, and not women standing for Parliament.

Food and trade routes. Asia, Americas. early modern.

In which year did Spanish ships first complete the return voyage across the Pacific from the Philippines to Mexico?

Answer 1565. Urdaneta's ship reaches Acapulco on 8 October 1565 Precision: day known (8 October); year used. Calendar: Gregorian.

The year of arrival at Acapulco of the first successful eastbound crossing (the tornaviaje). Not the year of the outward voyage and not the year Manila became the colony's capital. Event: the arrival that proved the return route. The route was then used until 1815.

Answer 1977. Diagnosis of Ali Maow Maalin in Somalia (October 1977), the last known natural case Precision: month (October); year used. Calendar: Gregorian.

The year of the last known naturally occurring case (the milder variola minor). Not the last case of any kind (a laboratory-acquired case in Birmingham in 1978) and not the year eradication was declared (1980). Event: the last natural case. Three other dates answer different questions: 1975 (last variola major case), 1978 (last case of any kind), 1980 (eradication declared).

Sounds for the people

A moment outside the usual chronology. Asia. medieval.

In which year was the Hunminjeongeum, the document that introduced the Korean alphabet, published?

Answer 1446. Publication of the Hunminjeongeum, about the ninth lunar month of 1446 (around October) Precision: month (ninth lunar month); year used. Calendar: Joseon lunisolar calendar, converted to Gregorian.

The year of publication. The alphabet was devised earlier: it was introduced to the court in the 12th lunar month of 1443, which spans 30 December 1443 to 28 January 1444 in the Gregorian calendar, so the creation year is ambiguous and is not used. Publication, not invention. Invention falls across a Gregorian year boundary (1443 or 1444).
