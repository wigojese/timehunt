# Project vision

*Status: user-fixed thesis from master prompt v2 (`TIMEHUNT_MASTER_PROMPT.md`). Nothing below has been playtested.*

## What TIMEHUNT is

A daily historical detective expedition. Each day has **ten different hidden-date mysteries** and **one shared bank of eight Insight** that does not refill between cases. Every case starts with free, sourced evidence. The player reasons about which years remain possible, may spend Insight on more evidence or a directional test (Trace), and commits a year with Lock. A wrong answer ends that case, never the day. The ten solved dates become a connected, explorable historical landscape and a World Reveal.

**Promise:** *Ten hidden moments. Eight chances to ask for help. How much of history can you uncover?*

## Names

- **TIMEHUNT** the product, **The Daily Ten** the daily mode
- **CONVERGENCE** the deduction engine (Evidence / Trace / Lock, provisional)
- **STRATA** the visual and spatial system (layers of time, Oxide & Bone)

## What the player should feel

- Local skill: reasoning from evidence, not recalling obscure dates and not blind number search.
- Long-horizon skill: deciding where to spend scarce help across ten cases.
- Pleasure: moving through layers of time, and seeing unexpected simultaneities at the end.
- No coercion: no paid hints, streak repair, energy timers or fake urgency.

## Fixed by the user (do not change without asking)

Ten cases per day. Eight shared Insight. Mobile-first and one-hand friendly. STRATA visuals, not blue or a grey dial. Evidence / Trace / Lock as the provisional grammar. Real path-scrolling and zoom. An interactive World Reveal, not slides. Broad, sourced, non-elite history including queer, everyday and statistical history. Sourcing and QA on every factual clue. Stage-gated delivery.

## What is not decided

Lock policy, Trace price and survival, clue prices, scoring formula, skip/return rules, daily reset convention, platform, final fonts, free-dossier size. These are settled by experiment and approval in Stages 2 to 5. See `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.

## Where the project is

Stage 0 (audit) complete and awaiting approval. There is no application code. The repo holds design boards, one single-case gray-box prototype and one simulation script. See `STAGE_0_AUDIT.md`.
