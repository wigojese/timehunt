STRATA 2.0 brand system

Layers, cuts and one red action

STRATA keeps its core idea: time is a stack of layers, evidence cuts them, and what the evidence rules out is hatched or stippled. Version 2.0 adds what ten cases need: a day strip, status marks that work without colour, and years that are large enough to press.

Texture carries only these four meanings. Status uses shape: solved, solved with help, missed, to come, always beside a word.
