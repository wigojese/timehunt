### Beat 3. Reading it took decades

*Interaction:* stonetime.

Sources (V1): [Wikimedia Foundation: Rosetta Stone (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosetta_Stone)

### Beat 4. How sure is the day?

Sources (V1): [Wikimedia Foundation: Rosetta Stone (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosetta_Stone)

*Interaction:* takeaway (editorial).

## The last case that was not a laboratory’s (1977, case 06)

Form: **Scrub a chart**. Where: Africa, Asia, South America.

### Beat 1. October 1977, Somalia

*Interaction:* text.

The last known naturally occurring case of smallpox was diagnosed in Somalia in October 1977. The CDC says there have been no cases since 1977.

Sources (V2): [Wikimedia Foundation: Ali Maow Maalin (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Maow_Maalin); [World Health Organization: History of smallpox vaccination](https://www.who.int/news-room/spotlight/history-of-vaccination/history-of-smallpox-vaccination); [US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention: About smallpox](https://www.cdc.gov/smallpox/about/index.html)

*Interaction:* smallpox.

The Intensified Smallpox Eradication Programme cost about US$300 million. WHO says two thirds came from the endemic countries’ own eradication efforts. Tap a part to read it.

## The way back across the Pacific (1565, case 03)

Form: **Follow a route**. Where: Mexico, the Philippines.

### Beat 1. 8 October 1565

*Interaction:* text.

Andrés de Urdaneta’s ship reached Acapulco on 8 October 1565. It had left Cebu on 1 June. Urdaneta and Alonso de Arellano made the first successful round trips that year, taking advantage of the Kuroshio Current.

Sources (V1): [Wikimedia Foundation: Andrés de Urdaneta (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_de_Urdaneta); [Wikimedia Foundation: Manila galleon (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_galleon)

### Beat 2. Out, and back

*Interaction:* route.

Choose a leg and move along it. The fleet sailed from La Navidad on 21 November 1564 and reached Samar on 13 February 1565. The way back left Cebu on 1 June 1565 and reached Acapulco on 8 October. The days are counted from those dates. The drawing shows no coastline and no track.

Sources (V1): [Wikimedia Foundation: Andrés de Urdaneta (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_de_Urdaneta)

### Beat 3. A route that lasted

*Interaction:* span.

The Manila galleon route ran from 1565 to 1815. The Spanish colonial capital moved to Manila in 1571. Tap a marker.

Sources (V1): [Wikimedia Foundation: Manila galleon (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_galleon); [Wikimedia Foundation: Miguel López de Legazpi (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_L%C3%B3pez_de_Legazpi)

### Beat 4. Finding the way back

*Interaction:* takeaway (editorial).

Urdaneta was given the task of finding a return route from the Philippines to Mexico. The Manila galleon route was inaugurated in 1565, once that return had worked, and 1565 is the year the case asks for.

Sources (V1): [Wikimedia Foundation: Andrés de Urdaneta (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9s_de_Urdaneta); [Wikimedia Foundation: Manila galleon (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manila_galleon)

Form: **Convert a calendar**. Where: Korea.

### Beat 1. About October 1446

*Interaction:* text.

The Hunminjeongeum, the document that introduced the Korean alphabet, was published around October 1446, in the ninth month of the Korean lunar calendar. The exact day is not known.
