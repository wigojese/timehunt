STRATA 1.5, from the second critique

One year, five worlds

The reveal was a slideshow of similar pages. Now every story has its own verb: inspect, read and compare, trace, scale. The look stays consistent; the hands do different things.

The entry is a survey grid instead of a list: five pins in date order, with the Moon and the world as off-grid stops. There are no coastlines, so positions are approximate and the screen says so.

Story 4 is in research: the first Pan-African Cultural Festival, Algiers, 21 July to 1 August 1969. Several sources agree on those dates. It stays a dashed pin until it has a real source record and rights-cleared material.

The Stonewall page shows no document because I will not fake one. It shows the slot, the provenance fields and the two tabs, so the design is honest about what content must arrive.

Exit is plain: play again, save to the archive, open sources, share. Nothing forces the four stories.
