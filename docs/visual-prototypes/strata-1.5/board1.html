STRATA 1.5, from the second critique

The hunt, with less machinery

One question at a time: the year, what is left, one control, one button. Guess history, evidence provenance and the fine-tune keys moved behind Details. Decade patterns are gone from the field; only the two ruled-out states keep a texture, and a lighter one. The clue now reads "true on 1 January of the year", which makes 1958 the first valid year and keeps the 27-year corridor honest. Previews describe a clue's power on your range and never where it cuts.
