STRATA 1.5, from the second critique

Motion you can read in stills

The critique's biggest gap was motion shown only as posters. These are storyboard frames of the three moments that carry the game: pinch-zoom, revealing a clue, and the solve. Each has a reduced-motion twin that keeps every piece of information.

Rule for all three: the camera never loses where you are. The year under your fingers stays put, and the view comes back exactly as you left it.

These frames are drawn, not recorded. Smoothness on a mid-range phone is still unproven and needs a running build.

Timings are design targets: feedback about 100 to 220 ms, redraw 250 to 450 ms, solve 500 to 900 ms and skippable.
