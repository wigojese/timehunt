# Consent script

Read aloud, in plain words. Give the participant a copy. Record "yes" or "no" for each line.

> Thank you for helping. I am testing a game about history. **I am testing the game, not you.** You cannot get anything wrong.
>
> **What happens.** You will play a game on a phone for about 25 minutes, saying aloud what you are thinking. Then I will ask some questions. Altogether about 50 minutes.
>
> **What I record.** The game saves which buttons you press and when. I take notes. This is stored under a code, not your name. You may say no to anything below and still take part.
>
> 1. May I **write notes** on what you say and do? Yes / No
> 2. May I **record your screen**? Yes / No
> 3. May I **record your voice**? Yes / No
> 4. May I **quote** things you said, without your name? Yes / No
>
> **Your data.** It stays with the project owner. It is not shared with other companies. Your name and contact details are kept separately and deleted within 30 days.
>
> **Your choices.** You can stop at any time and you do not have to say why. You can skip any question. You can ask me to delete your data afterwards, using the code on this sheet.
>
> **Content.** One case is about a queer-history institution and another about a disease. If you would like to skip a case, say "skip".
>
> **I am 18 or older.** Yes / No
>
> **I understand and agree to take part.** Yes / No
