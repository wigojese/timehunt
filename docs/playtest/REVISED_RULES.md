# Daily Ten rules, version 0.2 (provisional, not player-tested)

Changes from `docs/RULES_CANDIDATES.md` section 1 are in **bold**. The rule numbers and the plain wording are for a new player aged 13 or older.

> **TIMEHUNT: The Daily Ten**
> Every day has ten cases. In each case, one year is hidden.
> **1. Read the evidence.** Each piece of evidence rules some years out. The years still possible are listed for you. Reading is always free.
> **2. Use your Insight.** You have 8 Insight for the whole day, shared by all ten cases. It never refills. Spend 1 or 2 to reveal an extra clue. **A clue that tells you nothing new costs nothing.**
> **3. Lock a year.** You get one Lock per case. Right: solved. Wrong: you see the answer and why, and you move on. A miss never ends your day.
> **4. Every clue is true on 31 December of the hidden year.** "By the end of that year, X had happened" means X had happened by that date.
> **5. After ten cases,** you see your day.

What changed and why:
- **Rule 4 is new.** The end-of-year rule has been invisible in the game. The test (H1, quiz item 4) will say whether it is understood. Until then it is stated.
- **"The years still possible are listed for you"** replaces "slide the marker", because that is what the gray-box does.
- **Authoring rule L9 (not a player rule):** the cheaper clue in a case may not remove a subset of what the dearer clue removes, so the order in which you buy never matters.
- **Not changed:** one Lock; no Trace in the Daily; refund on redundant; solved cases are the score and Insight left is shown beside it.
- **Open:** whether the relation clue ("one calendar year after ...") is shown as evidence (A) or sold as a Deep clue (B) is untested (Q-32, Q-39).
