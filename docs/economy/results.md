## Experiment 7. The earlier "63 to 70% Trace-only" claim, reproduced and explained

Settings of the claim: 31 years, 8 Insight, Trace 3 (so 2 Traces), 2 Locks, no clues. The old simulator also gave every game one free opening card: a window of 20 consecutive years containing the target. Under the same generative model (M3) the exact numbers are:

| Case | Trace-only win probability |
|---|---|
| Exact ceiling 11/31 (any strategy, no card) | 35.5% |
| Exact optimal Trace-only, no free card | 35.5% |
| Midpoint-Trace rule, no free card | 35.5% |
| Exact optimal Trace-only WITH the old free 20-year card | 71.0% |

The old simulator's result (about 70% with the card, 35% without) is reproduced. The discrepancy was the undisclosed free card, not the Trace rules.

With Trace at 2 Insight and 10 Insight (5 Traces), the ceiling is 95 years, so 31 years are always won: 100.0%.
