# Content policy (stub, to be expanded in Stage 3)

Principles carried from the master prompt, not yet operationalised:
- No fabricated dates, statistics, quotes, identities or archival scans.
- Every clue has a source, a date precision, a dating convention and an editor sign-off.
- Separate source confidence, date precision, and whether a clue actually removes the years the game says it removes.
- Queer history appears as ordinary history, with attention to the terms people used then and the limits of the record. Not as shock or novelty.
- Statistics carry units, series, revision and uncertainty. No false precision.
- Coverage must be global, not mostly US and European.
No clue may be published under this policy until Stage 3 defines the review process and names an editor (Q-10).
